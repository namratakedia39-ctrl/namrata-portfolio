✦ The Journey

From Classroom Teacher
to Youth Transformer.

👩‍🏫

I spent years inside classrooms — first as a student, then as a teacher. And I kept seeing the same thing. Students studying. Scoring. Graduating. But when I asked them — 'Who are you beyond your marks?' They went quiet. Because nobody had ever asked them that question. Not once. In years of education.

💡

I know that silence personally. In my own academic journey, I felt no need to follow the crowd. I knew my potential. But under the weight of work pressure, daily routine, repeated tasks — I lost myself too. For years I was on autopilot. Doing. Not living. Working. Not growing. The day I recognized that — I made a decision.

🚀

I created AAKAR — not to change who students are, but to show them who they already are. And give them the tools to live from that place. To make them better by only 1% every day. Because that 1%, compounded daily, is the most powerful way to become the best version of yourself. From Akola, Maharashtra. With complete conviction.

🎯 "The goal is not to change who students are. The goal is to show them who they already are — and give them the tools to live from that place."
