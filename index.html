Effective Communication for Teachers & Leaders

"From Good to Great Communicator"

📌 Learning Objective

To develop essential communication skills that empower teachers and leaders to connect, influence, and lead with confidence in every interaction.

✅ After This Workshop, You Will:
Explain the 7-38-55 rule confidently
Demonstrate confident open body language
Apply volume, speed, tone & pause in class
Replace 5 negative phrases immediately
Respond — not react — in tough moments
Use the 3+1 formula in parent meetings
Give feedback using Sandwich Method
Apply TRUST formula daily
Construct open questions consistently
Practice 1% better communication every day
💡 "Great leaders are great communicators."
