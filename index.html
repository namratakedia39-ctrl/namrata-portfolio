My Story

Why I Do What I Do

Every mission begins with a moment of truth. Here is mine.

8+Certifications
✦ The Journey

From Classroom Teacher
to Youth Transformer.

👩‍🏫

I spent 5+ years as a teacher. I watched brilliant educators around me lose their creativity, their energy, their joy — buried under daily repetitive tasks and autopilot living. No one was telling them there was a better way.

💡

I tried to solve this by running workshops for teachers. But institutions were not ready to pay. Teachers were too busy. I hit a wall. So I pivoted — and discovered an even bigger crisis waiting: college students who had no idea who they were.

🚀

I asked 100+ students "Who Are You?" — and most went silent. That silence became my mission. I created AAKAR — a one-day, 100% activity-based workshop that helps students discover their identity, build confidence and set real goals. From Akola, Maharashtra — to every college that will listen.

🎯 "The goal is not to change who students are. The goal is to show them who they already are — and give them the tools to live from that place."
