✦ The Journey

From Teacher
to Teacher Trainer.

👩‍🏫

I started as a teacher myself — and I watched something that broke my heart. Teachers around me were giving their Sunday nights to school, carrying stress home, struggling with their mental health — not because they were bad at teaching, but because no one ever showed them a better way.

💡

The tools were always there — AI, communication frameworks, parent management strategies — but no one was making it accessible to teachers who needed it most. I realized the problem wasn't the teachers. It was the lack of awareness of how to do it differently.

🚀

So 5 months ago, I stood up and started. I began training teachers, college students, and professionals — anyone who wants to grow. My mission is simple: make people only 1% better every day — because that 1% compounds into transformation.

🎯 "Teachers are too busy — but few are having productive days. I'm here to change that."
